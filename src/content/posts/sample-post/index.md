## Quick Tips

- Add a title and description so posts look better in feeds.
- Use tags to organize your content.
- Keep `draft: true` while writing, then switch to `false` to publish.
- Set `visibility` to `site`, `social`, or `private` depending on how you want to share.

When you're ready, replace this with your own first post.

## Bluesky comments demo

The comment section below this post is a **live Bluesky thread** (Bluesky's own welcome post, wired up via the `blueskyUri` field so you can see it working). On your posts, the section finds your own share automatically — set your Bluesky handle in **Settings → AT Protocol / Bluesky**, cross-post as usual, and replies on Bluesky become comments here.
